DIRECTIONAL SENSING WITHOUT EXTRA SENSORS

Resolving left vs. right with iPhone mics.

How does a phone know if an obstacle is to your left or right? By measuring the fraction-of-a-millisecond time difference of arrival (Δt) between the iPhone's bottom and top microphones.

PHYSICAL PRINCIPLE

Time Difference of Arrival (TDOA)

Sound travels at ~343 mm/ms. Across the ~145mm baseline distance between the iPhone's bottom microphone and top earpiece microphone, an obstacle at an angle creates a measurable time offset:

Δt = (d_mics × sin θ) ÷ c

At 48,000 Hz, each audio sample is ~20.8 microseconds long. A 100-microsecond delay between microphones spans ~5 distinct samples, easily resolved via phase cross-correlation!

COREHAPTICS & 3D AUDIO

Communicating Direction to Blind Users

Echou never forces users to look at a screen. Instead, spatial information is translated into two hands-free sensory modes:

  • Taptic Engine Cadence: Rapid double-taps on the palm or chest indicate emergency stops; gentle periodic pulses indicate a safe forward buffer.
  • 3D Spatial Audio Beeps: When wearing AirPods or bone-conduction headphones, echoes are panned into binaural 3D space so the obstacle sounds like it's physically sitting in the room to your left or right.
  • Voice Announcements: Spoken distance summaries ("Wall 1.5 meters ahead, door to your right") via iOS speech synthesis.
DEVELOPMENT MILESTONES

Roadmap to the App Store.

Engineered by Parth Jaina, Vardaan Sharma, Manoj B S, and Tharun B with the blind and low-vision community.

01 Phase 1

Acoustic Calibration

Benchmarking 18–22 kHz speaker output and mic linearity across iPhone models. Calibrating direct speaker-to-mic leakage cancellation.

02 Phase 2

Accelerate DSP Core

Writing real-time FFT matched filtering and constant false alarm rate (CFAR) peak detection in Swift using Apple's Accelerate framework.

03 Phase 3

3-Mic TDOA Triangulation

Implementing multi-channel audio capture via AVAudioEngine to resolve Left, Center, and Right directional sectors.

04 Phase 4

Accessible UX & Haptics

Testing with blind users to refine Taptic Engine vibration rhythms, 3D audio cues, and voice feedback without sensory overload.

05 Phase 5

Public App & Spatial API

Releasing the free accessibility app on the App Store and publishing an open iOS Spatial Sonar SDK for third-party AR and navigation apps.
